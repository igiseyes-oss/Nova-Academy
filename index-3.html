About Us

About Nova Academy

Nova Academy is an online educational institute that has spent the past three years working toward a single goal: making practical, skill-based learning accessible to youth, regardless of their background or budget. What began as a small effort to teach useful digital skills has grown into a structured academy offering courses across digital skills, business, creative work, language learning and technology.

Every course is designed to be learned on your own schedule, through recorded lectures, live sessions or guided WhatsApp classes — because we believe access shouldn't depend on timing or location. Many of our courses are offered free of cost or at a minimal fee, reflecting our core mission: empowering youth with skills, not creating barriers to learning.

Nova Academy is led by Zobia Maluk, whose vision continues to shape the academy's focus on practical outcomes — a skill you can use, a portfolio you can show, and a certificate you can rely on.
